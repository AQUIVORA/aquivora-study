Quick Revision

Key ideas and exam checklist.

⚡ Quick Revision

  • Every composite number has a unique prime factorisation apart from order.
  • Rational number: p/q, q ≠ 0.
  • Irrational number: cannot be expressed as p/q.
  • For HCF use the lowest powers of common prime factors.
  • For LCM use the highest powers of all prime factors.

⭐ Exam Checklist

  • Prime factorisation
  • HCF and LCM
  • Rational vs irrational numbers
  • Proof by contradiction

Common Mistakes

  • Not every square-root expression is irrational.
  • In contradiction proofs, state that p/q is in lowest terms.
