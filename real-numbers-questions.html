AQUIVORA • CLASS 10 • CHAPTER 01

Important Questions

Questions divided by marks.

1 Mark

  1. State the Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic.
  2. Is √7 rational or irrational?
  3. Write 84 as a product of primes.

2 Marks

  1. Find the prime factorisation of 180.
  2. Explain why √5 is irrational.
  3. Give an example showing two irrational numbers can have a rational product.

3 Marks

  1. Find the HCF and LCM of 72 and 120 using prime factorisation.
  2. Prove that √2 is irrational.
  3. Explain why irrational numbers are not closed under every operation.

5 Marks

  1. Prove that √3 is irrational using contradiction.
  2. Explain how HCF and LCM are obtained from prime factorisations.
  3. Find the HCF and LCM of 96 and 144 and explain your method.
